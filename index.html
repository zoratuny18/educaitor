Create complete lesson plans, activities and printable worksheets, aligned to the curriculum for your subject and grade.

  • Classroom-ready activities and printable worksheets.
  • Curriculum alignment built into the planning process.

Assess one student at a time or upload a whole batch. Then bring the results together to see what your class understands, where they need support and what may need reteaching.

Why We Do What We Do

Teaching asks for more than a school day.

Lesson planning after dinner. Marking on Sundays. Parent calls, classroom dynamics, emotional support and endless decisions. Teachers carry the work of several professions, often without the time or recognition that work deserves.

EducAItor pro is a first investment in teachers. By taking repetitive work off their plates, we give them more room for the human work that matters.

Q&A

Your Questions, Answered

What to know before you get started.

Who can use EducAItor pro?

EducAItor pro is exclusively for K–12 teachers. Administrators, parents and students are not platform users.

What tools are included?

Lesson Planner, Smart Grading, Pedagogical Support and Personal Space. Each tool is designed around a different part of a teacher’s work.

Does Smart Grading decide the grade?

No. EducAItor pro assists with assessment and analysis. The teacher reviews the results and always makes the final grading decision.

Can I assess more than one student at a time?

Yes. Teachers can assess students individually or upload multiple student assessments in a batch. Batch completion triggers an email notification.

Where can I download the app?

Download EducAItor pro from the Apple App Store.
