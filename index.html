TEACHER SPOTLIGHT

Kari

Kindergarten Teacher & Mama · Classroom Cheerleader · Animal Lover · @itsmrsnorris

Kari has given thirteen years to this profession, teaching reading, 4th, 1st, 2nd, and now her beloved kindergarten, so she knows every corner of what this job asks of you. She is a mama of three, an animal lover with a dog and a cat of her own, and she still finds time to volunteer at her local animal shelter because caring for others is simply who she is. In her classroom she is the cheerleader every child remembers, the one who makes the little ones feel big and brave.
