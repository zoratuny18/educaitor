Lesson Planner

Create complete lesson plans, classroom activities and differentiated worksheets in seconds. Curriculum alignment and Common Core standards are built in by default, tailored to your location, grade and subject matter.

  • Curriculum aligned
  • Printable
  • Differentiation options in one click

02 / Smart Grading

I was skeptical, then I graded a whole stack from my phone on the ride home

01

Photograph student work

Take photos directly with your phone camera. No scanner or complicated upload process.

02

Assess one student or a whole batch

Grade each student individually or upload multiple student assessments together. We notify you by email when grading of the entire batch is complete.

03

Review

You will get a comprehensive assessment of the uploaded work and a suggested grade. The final call is always yours. Use what resonates. Done.

See the class picture

Individual results feed into class analytics, revealing patterns, learning gaps and questions or topics many students struggled with.

Student privacy matters. We do not collect or store personal information about students. Submitting identifiable student information is not permitted on the platform.

Q&A

Your Questions, Answered

What to know before you get started.

Who can use EducAItor pro?

EducAItor pro is exclusively for K–12 teachers. Administrators, parents and students are not platform users.

What tools are included?

Lesson Planner, Smart Grading, Pedagogical Support and Personal Space. Each tool is designed around a different part of a teacher’s work.

Does Smart Grading decide the grade?

No. EducAItor pro assists with assessment and analysis. The teacher reviews the results and always makes the final grading decision.

Can I assess more than one student at a time?

Yes. Teachers can assess students individually or upload multiple student assessments in a batch. Batch completion triggers an email notification.

Where can I download the app?

Download EducAItor pro from the Apple App Store.
